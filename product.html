Technology & Safety

Thermal Safety at the Atomic Level

A cell-to-cell thermal barrier isolates every cell, so heat cannot spread if one cell is damaged. This thermal runaway containment keeps a single fault from becoming a pack-level event, while the liquid cooling plate keeps the whole pack in its ideal temperature range.
